MessageBackup

Export iPhone Messages to XML on your Mac

MessageBackup helps you browse your iPhone Messages database, preview conversations, and export a single thread as XML — entirely on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to sign in and sync your export count.

  • Browse your local chat.db file safely
  • Preview conversations before exporting
  • Extract one contact thread to XML
  • Optional account sign-in to track exports

You can sign in later from the bottom of the left menu.

How it works

  1. Select your Messages chat.db file
  2. Load contacts from the database snapshot
  3. Preview a conversation with a contact
  4. Export that thread as XML to your chosen folder

Privacy first

The local desktop app only reads files you choose. Conversion happens on your Mac. Sign-in is optional and opens the hosted dashboard only if you want account sync.
