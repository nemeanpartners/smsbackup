SMSBackup

Welcome. Back up your iPhone Messages securely and locally.

Start backup & sync

In one guided session, SMSBackup will safely prepare a conversation backup on your Mac and sync it to your profile.

This session will:

  1. Open the SMSBackup web app so you can sign in.
  2. Find your Messages database and let you choose a conversation.
  3. Save that conversation and upload it to your account.

Nothing is uploaded until you confirm, and you can stop at any time.

Backup setup

Follow the steps below to find your Messages database, pick a conversation, and export it as an Android‑ready XML backup.
