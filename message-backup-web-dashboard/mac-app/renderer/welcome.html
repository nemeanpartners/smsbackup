Clarified

Welcome. Back up your iPhone Messages and analyze your conversations securely, locally, and calmly.

Get your iPhone Messages ready for Clarified

In one guided session, Clarified will safely prepare a conversation backup on your Mac and sync it to your Clarified profile for analysis.

This session will:

  1. Open the Clarified web app so you can sign in.
  2. Find your Messages database and let you choose a conversation.
  3. Save that conversation and upload it to Clarified for insights and reports.

Nothing is uploaded until you confirm, and you can stop at any time.
